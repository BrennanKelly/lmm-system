# Ideal Client Profile — Lake Michigan Marketing

> Defines who LMM sells to. Every prospect is scored against this framework before outreach begins. Load at boot.

## Who We Serve

LMM works with local service businesses that:
- Need a consistent flow of new customer leads
- Have high customer lifetime value (makes Google Ads ROI-positive)
- Operate in a defined geographic area
- Can handle increased volume when we deliver leads

| Vertical | Why It Works | Avg Job Value |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Roofing | High ticket, high urgency, Google-first buying behavior | $5,000–$15,000 |
| HVAC | Repeat customers, high urgency, seasonal volume spikes | $2,000–$8,000 |
| Landscaping | Recurring revenue, high volume, underserved in paid search | $500–$5,000/yr |
| Gym / Fitness | Monthly recurring revenue, strong LTV | $600–$1,200/yr |

## Ideal Business Attributes

| Attribute | Ideal | Minimum |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| Years in business | 3+ years | 1 year |
| Google Reviews | 20+ reviews, 4.0+ rating | 5+ reviews |
| Service area | 1–3 cities or 20–50 mile radius | Single city |
| Monthly ad budget | $1,000–$3,000 | $500 |
| Owner availability | Responds to leads within 1 hour | Same day |
| Website | Exists (even basic) | None required (we build the page) |

## Buy Signals (Reach Out Now)

- Running Google Ads inconsistently or poorly — opportunity to take over
- Relies primarily on referrals and wants to diversify
- Has a slow season coming and wants leads before it hits
- Just lost a marketing agency or in-house person
- Recently expanded service area or added a new service
- Posting on social media asking for more business

## ICP Scoring (8 Points)

| # | Criterion | 1 Point If... |
|---|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | Right vertical | Roofing, HVAC, landscaping, gym, or approved other |
| 2 | Right market | City with 50,000+ population, or suburb of major metro |
| 3 | Google presence | Google Business Profile exists with reviews |
| 4 | Ads opportunity | Running bad ads, ran before, or not running but should be |
| 5 | No competing agency | Not currently locked in with another lead gen agency |
| 6 | Budget signal | Business scale suggests $500+/mo available |
| 7 | Reachable decision maker | Owner or GM identifiable and contactable |
| 8 | Clear need signal | Few reviews, weak ranking, or slow season approaching |

**Score 6–8**: Priority. Full 12-day outreach sequence.
**Score 4–5**: Secondary. Single message + monitor.
**Score < 4**: Pass. Not worth pursuing now.

## Anti-Patterns (Do Not Pursue)

- Businesses under 1 year old — unstable, hard to get results for
- No interest in building an online presence
- Wants “social media management only” — not LMM’s core service
- Average job value under $300 — Google Ads economics don’t work
- Owner philosophically opposed to paid advertising

## What Client Success Looks Like

1. CPL within target range for their vertical
2. Receiving at least 10 qualified leads per month
3. Converting leads to booked jobs at a rate that makes the economics work
4. Renews after the first 90 days

## ICP Review Cadence

Review and update quarterly based on:
- Which verticals produce the best CPL and retention
- Which market sizes (cities) perform best
- Which client budget tiers are easiest to get results for
- Client feedback on lead quality
