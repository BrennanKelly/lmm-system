for local service businesses.

**What’s included**:
| Deliverable | What It Is |
|-------------|------------|
| Google Ads | High-intent campaigns targeting buyers in your service area |
| Landing Page | Conversion-optimized page matched to your ads |
| GoHighLevel CRM | Pipeline, contact tracking, reporting — all set up for you |
| SMS + Email Automation | Leads followed up within 2 minutes, automatically |
| Weekly Reports | What’s working, what we’re testing, what’s next |

**Pricing**:
| Tier | Ad Budget | Management Fee | Best For |
|------|-----------|---------------|----------|
| Starter | $500–$999/mo | $750/mo | Testing Google Ads for the first time |
| Growth | $1,000–$2,499/mo | $1,000/mo | Established business wanting consistent leads |
| Scale | $2,500+/mo | $1,500+/mo | Multi-location or aggressive growth |

Setup fee: $500 one-time. Waived for 3-month commitment.

**Guarantee**:
> “If we don’t generate at least 10 leads in your first 30 days,
> we work for free until we do.”
